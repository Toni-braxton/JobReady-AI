# JobReady AI 🎯

### AI-Powered CV and Job Matching Assistant

JobReady AI is an AI-powered application designed to help Kenyan students, recent graduates, and entry-level job seekers tailor their CVs to specific job advertisements.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Job seekers often spend hours customizing their CVs for different positions. JobReady AI aims to simplify this process by identifying relevant skills, suggesting improvements, and highlighting missing job requirements.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Users provide their CV and a job description. The AI analyzes both and generates a report containing relevant strengths, suggested CV improvements, and skills or qualifications that may need attention.

## ✨ Planned Features

- CV and job description matching
- Relevant skills identification
- AI-generated CV improvement suggestions
- Missing requirements analysis
- Accuracy and privacy safeguards
- User-reviewed recommendations

## 👥 Target Users

- University and college students
- Recent graduates
- Entry-level job seekers in Kenya
- Internship applicants

## 🤖 AI Safety and Responsible Use

- Prevent fabricated work experience and qualifications.
- Protect personally identifiable information (PII).
- Focus recommendations on job-related skills.
- Require users to review AI-generated suggestions.

## 🚀 Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first version will generate a CV-to-job match report showing relevant strengths, suggested CV bullet improvements, and requirements not clearly demonstrated in the CV.

## 📊 Success Metrics

- At least 70% of pilot users prepare a job-specific CV draft within 10 minutes.
- At least 80% rate the suggestions as relevant and factually accurate.
- Track and minimize unsupported AI-generated claims.
